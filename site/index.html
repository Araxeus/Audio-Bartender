Audio Bartender
Features Native or Web
FOR WINDOWS 10 & 11

The volume mixer Windows was missing.

It lives in your tray. Set each app's volume, send apps to different speakers, and choose which app your media keys control.

Download for Windows Portable versions
Free · 6 MB installer · No account needed

Try it. This is the real app, running in your browser. Click the tray icon, scroll it, right-click an app, open settings.

Your media keys. Press one.

Everything audio, one click from the tray

Open it from the tray icon. Close it by clicking anywhere else.

Media keys, routed

Play/pause goes to the app you pick, not whichever tab grabbed it last.

Per-app volume

Every app gets a slider and a live level meter. Scroll to adjust.

Per-app output

Music on headphones, the game on speakers. Right-click an app to move it.

Now playing

Album art, track info and controls for whatever is playing.

A tray icon that listens

Waves, rings and pulses that move with the music. Scroll it for volume, middle-click to mute.

Yours to tune

Light or dark, 14 accent colors, and hide any part you don't use.

Press play, and the right app answers

Windows sends media keys to whichever app spoke up last. Audio Bartender lets you choose.

  1. Pick an appClick it under Media keys go to.
  2. Press any media keyPlay, pause, next and previous go there, even with a video open in your browser.
  3. Close it wheneverThe keys go back to Windows until the app starts again.
